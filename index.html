Hi! I’m a Ph.D. student at AIRIS Lab, KAIST.

I’m interested in making virtual objects and spaces feel coherent, interactive, and alive through sound.

My research connects psychoacoustics, auditory AR/VR, spatial audio, and generative tools. Recently, I have been focusing on how people expect virtual objects to sound—and how those expectations can become responsive sound behavior.
