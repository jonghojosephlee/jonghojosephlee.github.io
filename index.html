About

Hi! I’m a Ph.D. student at AIRIS Lab, KAIST. My research explores auditory perception and sound expectations in XR, especially how virtual and meta-objects should sound as people interact with them. I’m interested in spatial audio and AI-based sound generation, with a recent focus on creating sound behaviors that respond to object properties, user actions, and changing virtual contexts.
